Glucose 4.1 shows two subtitles at once: live subtitles with a subtitle file, two languages together, or a translation with its original.

## What's new

### Two subtitles at once
- The subtitles menu has a Big line and a Small line. Either one can show live subtitles, the live original, a track embedded in the video, or a subtitle file.
- Use it for live subtitles together with a subtitle file, two subtitle files in different languages, or a live translation with the original underneath.
- The small line keeps its space while it has nothing to show, so the big line stays put.
- Each line has its own timing, in steps of 0.25 seconds, for subtitle files that are a little early or late.
- Shift+C hides or shows the small line. Alt+C swaps the two lines.

### Fix a line where you see it
- Rest the mouse on a subtitle line to swap it with the other line, shift its timing or turn it off. Everything there is also in the subtitles menu.

### Subtitles named by language
- Subtitle tracks and files are listed by language, read from the embedded track or the file name (for example "Movie.en.srt", "Movie.pt-BR.srt" or "2_English.srt").
- Files without a language in their name are listed by their name.

### Keep watching while subtitles generate
- Generating subtitles for a whole video no longer covers the player. Progress and Cancel are at the top of the subtitles menu.

### Subtitle style
- The style panel has a Small line section for the small line's size and color.
- A preset's own text color, such as Highlight's dark text, is offered as a swatch, so it can be picked again after trying another color.

## Changed
- Live subtitles, generating for the whole video and importing a file are under Generate / Import in the subtitles menu. Live subtitles are turned off there, or by choosing None for their line.
- "Show original subtitles too" (Settings → AI) now decides whether the original goes on the small line when live translation starts. Either line can be changed from the subtitles menu afterwards.
- Live subtitles stop as soon as neither line shows them.

## Good to know
- Line choices and timing are kept for the video that is open, and start fresh for the next one.
